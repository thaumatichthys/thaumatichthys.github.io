Experimental Direct Sequence Spread Spectrum demodulator in C++

June 2025

This page is a bit of a stub, but I tried to make a direct sequence spread spectrum demodulator in C++. It takes float inputs and attemps to spit out the encoded bitstream.

This demodulator doesn't work very well, but it was helpful in teaching how a few things work in the topics of DSP.

  • DSSS Demodulator, despreading, LPF + power integration for despread detection
  • IIR filters
  • Costas loop for tracking signal phase
  • Digital PLLs, loop filters, NCO
  • Bitstream clock recovery (with PLL or with high-Q filter + zero crossing detector)
  • Pulse shaping and zero-ISI filters (raised cosine filter or 2x convolution of square root raised cosine)

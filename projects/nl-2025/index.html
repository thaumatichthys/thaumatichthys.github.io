ESA Soldering course and Netherlands trip 2025

May 18-23, 2025

In 2025, I was fortunate enough to be part of the European Space Agency's soldering course for the Fly Your Satellite 4 teams. Ada Li and I represented ALEASAT, the joint project between the UBC Orbit and SFU Satellite design teams. The event lasted two days, but I decided to stay in the Netherlands for three extra days.

ESA FYS4 Soldering Course

The event took place at ESA's ESTEC facility, just north of Leiden, Netherlands. During this event, we were taught basic soldering concepts relating to high reliability, flight qualified solder joints. We have compiled some basic notes below.

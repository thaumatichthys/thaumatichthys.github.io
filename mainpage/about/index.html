About

Hello! My name is Max Xiang and I am currently an undergraduate student at the University of British Columbia. I have an interest in electronic design and I am studying electronic engineering, but my interests span other subjects such as mechanical design, 3D printing, insects, and more!

I made this site in the last year of high school to document some of my projects. I do this mostly to remind myself of how I did them, in case I need to revisit them in the future. I also hope to provide a resource for learning how to do certain things, or how various things work. Note that this is not an exhaustive list; I often only write projects that I actually complete, which is probably like around 50% of all the ones I attempt. If you see any errors of have questions, please feel free to contact me.
